Slide to pay, rebuilt in Liquid Glass

The production control is a flat blue capsule with a white knob and a sheen that sweeps the track every 2.6 seconds. This version keeps that cadence and rebuilds the control as layered glass: a floating tray over the checkout, a tinted capsule, and a clear lens knob that bends the label as it passes over it.

Material stack

  • Floating tray

    Total, control and legal line sit on one glass sheet inset 12 px from the screen edge. blur 20 · saturate 170% · white 60%, 34 px radius, soft 22 px shadow.

  • Tinted capsule

    Sam’s blue carried as a tint over the blurred backdrop rather than a flat fill, so scrolling content shifts the colour slightly. blur 14 · saturate 180% · blue 82→74%.

  • Rim light

    Light arrives from the top left: a 1 px white inner highlight on the top edge, a deeper blue inner shadow on the bottom edge, and a radial specular at 18% / 0%.

  • Lens knob

    Clear glass instead of solid white. In Chrome and Edge the knob refracts its backdrop through an SVG displacement map, so the label bends inside it as it slides. Elsewhere a gradient ring stands in for the refraction.

  • Sheen

    The production sweep, kept at its 2.6 s cadence, rendered as a soft caustic in screen blend. It pauses while you hold the knob.

States

  • IdleSheen sweeps. Knob rests at the left edge.
  • PressedCapsule scales to 1.015 and the knob brightens.
  • DraggingKnob trails the finger slightly and stretches with velocity. The label fades as it is covered. A lighter liquid fill follows behind.
  • Released earlyBelow 88%, the knob springs back with a small overshoot.
  • CompleteKnob swells leftward to fill the capsule and dissolves. Tint turns positive green and the label reads “Order placed”.

Try it: drag the knob to the end, release it early to see the spring, and scroll the checkout underneath the tray. Keyboard: focus the knob, then arrow keys move it and End completes.
