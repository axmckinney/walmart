Checkout · prototype

Slide to pay, rebuilt in Liquid Glass

The production control is a flat blue capsule with a white knob and a sheen that sweeps the track every 2.6 seconds. This version rebuilds it as layered glass: a floating tray over the checkout, a solid Club Blue capsule kept flat as in production, and glass reserved for two parts: a clear lens knob that bends the label as it passes over it, and the label itself. The shimmer moves off the capsule and onto the label, at the cadence of the iOS 27 slide controls. Type is Sam’s Sans UI and colour comes from the brand playbook. The same control is written as SwiftUI components in the repo.

Material stack

  • Floating tray

    Total, control and legal line sit on one glass sheet inset 12 px from the screen edge. blur 20 · saturate 170% · white 60%, 34 px radius, soft 22 px shadow.

  • Solid capsule

    Club Blue #0A6CFF, flat, with no gradient and no material, exactly as production paints it. Keeping the track solid lets the two glass parts read as glass.

  • Rim light

    On the knob only. Light arrives from the top left: a 1 px white inner highlight on its upper edge, a deeper blue inner shadow on its lower edge, and a specular at 30% / 24%.

  • Lens knob

    Clear glass instead of solid white. In Chrome and Edge the knob refracts its backdrop through an SVG displacement map, so the label bends inside it as it slides. Elsewhere a gradient ring stands in for the refraction.

  • Label shimmer

    Borrowed from the iOS 27 slide controls: a soft band about a third of the label’s width crosses it once every 1.6 s, brightening the text from 62% white to white. The capsule itself stays still. It pauses while you hold the knob.

  • Brand assets

    Sam’s Sans UI throughout: Semibold for the label and total, Medium for chips, Regular for body, Mono UI for the values on this page. Completion turns Maverick Green #00AC44. The logomark and wordmark are the supplied SVGs, not retyped.

States

  • IdleLabel shimmers. Knob rests at the left edge.
  • PressedCapsule scales to 1.015 and the knob brightens.
  • DraggingKnob trails the finger slightly and stretches with velocity. The label fades as it is covered. A lighter liquid fill follows behind.
  • Released earlyBelow 88%, the knob springs back with a small overshoot.
  • ProcessingKnob parks at the end and its chevron becomes a progress ring. The label reads “Placing your order” and keeps shimmering, a touch faster, until the payment service answers. Simulated here as 1.6 s.
  • CompleteKnob swells leftward to fill the capsule and dissolves. Tint turns Maverick Green and the label reads “Order placed”.

Try it: drag the knob to the end, release it early to see the spring, and scroll the checkout underneath the tray. Keyboard: focus the knob, then arrow keys move it and End completes.
